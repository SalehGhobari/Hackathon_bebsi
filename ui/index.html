    What this means

    Every year a working laptop stays in service avoids a share of the carbon and mining that a new one would require. The cheapest repair is usually also the greenest choice — that is the whole point.

    Where it actually goes

    Jordan · contact details from the recyclers

    Recycling is the last resort, and only useful if the route is real. Some recyclers collect from your location: call the mobile number, give a rough count and send photos. Confirm accepted items, fees and timing when you call.

    Second-life passport

    A portable record of what was measured, what was repaired, and what was avoided. Useful when selling a used machine — or when buying one.

    Data destruction

    Before a device is resold or recycled, the drive matters more than the hardware. Record the method you used. Fahas stores an attestation locally — it is a record, not a cryptographic proof.
